01

Profile

I am a VDC coordinator and Autodesk developer in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina, with about twenty five years in land development, most of it in AutoCAD Civil 3D, moving projects from raw survey through construction documents and closeout. That work has taken me coast to coast: nine years in California, three in New Mexico, a year in Arkansas training users across Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi and Texas, and years in Georgia and the Carolinas. I have trained people online in more states than I can count.

Through New Roots Development, my own practice since 2018 and a South Carolina LLC since July 2026, I help businesses and individuals put AI to work in the way they already work, and I train people to run it the way I learned to teach as an Autodesk Authorized Instructor. I am an FAA Part 107 remote pilot and a certified drone maintenance technician through Horry Georgetown Technical College.

Earlier I ran CADTECHS, my own Autodesk training and consulting practice, spent four years supporting Autodesk and its reseller channel as an AEC applications specialist across twelve states, wrote The Civil Side for AUGI, and grew the largest Civil 3D user group on LinkedIn past ten thousand members before selling it. Before CAD I framed houses, ran a forklift, fabricated steel, and programmed CNC machines. That is still how I read a set of plans.
